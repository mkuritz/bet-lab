Signal scoreboard (the feedback loop)

Each pick is tagged with the signals behind it. A signal becomes PROVEN after ≥ graded picks with positive ROI and non-negative CLV, and LOSING if ROI or CLV is negative. Bet Lab ranks with these weights: proven signals rank higher, losing ones rank lower or get hidden, and weights stay near 1.0 until the sample is large.
