Sports Labs Recommended vs research

Cream rises to the top. A pick counts as SPORTS LABS RECOMMENDED only when it clears a strict, rules-based gate before the game starts (tiered before the game starts; the pick log itself stays append-only):
  • Only our highest-conviction picks, where our proven signals agree and we beat the market price.
  • Every Recommended pick is priced at a real market and locked in before the game.
  • A small daily handful per sport, so only the cream rises to the top.
Everything else is RESEARCH ONLY: still logged and graded, shown for curiosity, and never counted in the official Edge Tracker record. Both rule-based and model-based picks are labeled. Misses stay on the board.

🎯 Called it

After grading, we auto-generate cards for Recommended picks that hit, with the real stat line that night and the signals that flagged them. Game-script calls (shootout/over, grinder/under) logged pre-game get the same treatment. Calls made in chat are shown as “Called in chat” and never count toward the official record.

Stars Align & upside plays

A longshot, parlay or boosted bet gets ⭐ STARS ALIGN only when it's +EV at our estimate and at least 2 independent signals with a proven record (≥ graded picks, positive ROI and CLV) back it. Suggested stakes stay small relative to bankroll. Everything else is FUN MONEY · NOT +EV.
